What we do

HMU helps learners access information and guidance on universities, colleges, bursaries, scholarships, and student support programmes.

Study Pathways

We help learners understand the options available after Grade 12, from universities and colleges to skills-based programmes.

Bursaries & Scholarships

Learners can find support and information on funding opportunities that make education more accessible and affordable.

School & Community Support

HMU works with schools and partners to reach young people who need practical, reliable guidance for their future.

Support that makes a difference

Many learners do not know where to start when they finish matric. HMU works to remove the confusion and give them clear, practical next steps.

  • Clear information about institutions and study options
  • Guidance on financial aid and bursary opportunities
  • Support for learners who need extra encouragement and direction

Why learners use HMU

We are committed to helping young people find the information they need without feeling overwhelmed by endless searches and uncertainty.
