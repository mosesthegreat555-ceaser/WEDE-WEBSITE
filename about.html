About Help Me Up

Practical information and support for learners making the move after Matric.

Our story

Help Me Up started because many learners leave school unsure what to do next. We registered in 2025 and began running support in 2026 to help students access clear information about study and funding options.

We work simply: share helpful information, run school sessions, and point learners to the next practical step.

Our purpose

We make post-school education information easier to find and understand so learners can make choices that suit them.

  • Support young people after matric
  • Share information about study and funding opportunities
  • Work with schools and communities to reach learners

Our values

Everything we do is guided by the belief that every learner deserves a fair chance to succeed.

Access

We believe learners should have access to the information that can change their future.

Growth

We encourage learners to grow, learn, and discover their strengths and potential.

Support

We create practical steps and encouragement to help learners move forward.

Opportunity

We focus on useful, realistic ways learners can continue studying or train for work.

Our journey

2025 HMU was officially registered as a non-profit organisation.
2026 Operations began and support services for Grade 12 learners were launched.
Today We continue to support learners through school partnerships, guidance and outreach.

Our mission

To give learners the straightforward information and support they need to plan their next move after Matric.

Our vision

A future where every learner can access the guidance needed to continue learning or prepare for work.
